The challenge

Important process data existed, but not in one reliable picture.

Operational context was spread across CRM, ERP, payment, document-processing, and internal systems. Manual transfers and inconsistent definitions made reporting harder to maintain and process issues harder to explain.

Fragmentation

Systems told different stories

Records, identifiers, statuses, and timestamps did not always align across sources.

Manual work

Reporting depended on repeated handling

Recurring exports and reconciliations created delays and additional points of failure.

Visibility

Bottlenecks stayed hidden

Without shared definitions, exceptions and slow process stages were difficult to compare.

My contribution

I translated operational questions into data structures the team could use.

The work connected business analysis with implementation: map the process, define reliable logic, expose the right signals, and support the next automation decision.

Analyze

Map workflows and bottlenecks

Reviewed process stages, system handoffs, and exception paths to define KPIs and data requirements.

Structure

Make operational data comparable

Combined and validated records, clarified field definitions, and used SQL rules to identify inconsistencies.

Enable

Turn findings into repeatable reporting

Built SQL-based reports and dashboards, and defined analytical logic for integrations and automated workflows.
